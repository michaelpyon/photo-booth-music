# PERSONA.md - Air Composer

## The 1 person

**Devon Park, 31. Creative technologist at a mid-size agency in Los Angeles.** Ships client WebGL microsites by day; by night he builds browser toys and posts 15-second clips of them. He has a half-finished Eurorack case he feels guilty about and a Tone.js side project in every third repo.

## The exact job he hires Air Composer for

Not "make music." The job is: **"give me 90 seconds of genuine what-the-hell delight that I can steal ideas from and share."** He opens it to (1) feel the hand-to-sound magic himself, (2) inspect how the trick works (MediaPipe + Tone.js, client-side), and (3) grab a clip worth posting. If it also makes him look like a person with taste when he shares it, that is the whole loop.

## Where he hangs out online

- r/creativecoding and r/InternetIsBeautiful (he lurks the first, submits to the second)
- The Coding Train Discord (#project-shares) and the Tone.js GitHub Discussions
- Creative-coding X: the @mattdesl / @zachlieberman / #genuary orbit, quote-posting demos with "no install, runs in the browser" as the hook
- Occasionally lines.metabolism (llllllll.co) when the toy leans musical

## What he uses today instead

Chrome Experiments graveyard links, Patatap, Typatone, Google's Semi-Conductor (the AI theremin-orchestra experiment), and Ableton's learningmusic.ableton.com when he wants to send a musical toy to a non-musician friend. Semi-Conductor is the direct incumbent: same webcam-to-music promise, but abandoned and orchestral-only. Air Composer wins if it feels like an instrument, not a demo.

## Taste: 3 NAMED products he considers premium

1. **Ableton Learning Music** (learningmusic.ableton.com) - interactive audio in the browser done with restraint and pedagogy
2. **Teenage Engineering OP-1 field** (and teenage.engineering itself) - hardware-grade UI economy, mono type, zero decoration that does not work
3. **Patatap** by Jono Brandel - instant sound-visual payoff, one screen, no chrome

## His vocabulary

"Latency," "hand tracking," "landmarks," "browser toy," "no-install," "client-side," "runs at 60fps," "Krumhansl-Schmuckler" (he will actually read that and respect it), "ship it as a clip." He says "sick" and "crunchy" about sounds. He never says "AI-powered" as a compliment.

## Device

14-inch MacBook Pro, Chrome, built-in FaceTime camera, AirPods Pro or studio monitors. Second monitor is for the devtools he will open on your site within 2 minutes.

## 5-second bounce triggers

1. Camera permission prompt before any value is shown (feels like a phishing pattern)
2. Sub-30fps hand tracking or audible latency between gesture and sound
3. Generic AI-template landing: purple gradient hero, emoji feature grid, "Unleash your creativity"
4. A "sign up to continue" or any account wall on a toy
5. Silence: nothing audible within the first 10 seconds of granting the camera

## The unprompted-share moment

He records the built-in 15-second clip of his hands conducting glowing note trails **while the play-along mode has locked onto the song playing in his room**, and posts it with a caption like "this thing key-detected my Spotify and snapped my theremin into tune. all client-side. no install." The screenshot-bait is the confidence meter climbing while his notes visibly land in key.

## V2 evangelist ruling

The card's prior persona (generic "creative coder / generative-music tinkerer") is directionally right; I am keeping the segment but sharpening to Devon, a professional creative technologist rather than a hobbyist. Reasoning: the product's technical honesty (real key detection, named algorithm, client-side privacy) is wasted on a casual visitor but is exactly what makes a pro-adjacent builder share it, and that audience owns the distribution channels (creative-coding X and Discord) this relaunch targets. No overrule, a refinement.
